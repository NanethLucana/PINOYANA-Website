OUR SIGNATURE TREAT

GINATAANG PALITAW

Ginataang Palitaw is a Filipino delicacy made with soft and chewy rice balls in rich and creamy coconut milk. PINOYANA offers Original and Innovated variants that bring a delightful twist to this traditional Filipino favorite at an affordable price.

🌼 Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a delicious, affordable, and satisfying Filipino treat while sharing the goodness of local delicacies and creating a memorable experience for every customer.
